# Boletín UDES en Cifras (documento A4 imprimible)

Scope: the A4 document that the bulletin renderer draws (cover, presentation and contents, chapter per report, indicator units, dataset units, back cover), how it shows on screen (paged preview in the intranet and in the public portal), and how it prints to PDF. The template editor is a sixth station inside the UDES en Cifras intranet.
Visitor mode: Read. Readers are directors, accreditation peers and the public who get the PDF. The editor is Operate and inherits the intranet brief.

Audience and job: Planeación puts together a global bulletin, or one per report, from the figures already loaded, sets texts, images, colors and font, chooses what goes in and in what order, then downloads the PDF. It can publish the live version on the web and freeze PDF editions. The reader needs to understand each indicator (what it measures, who reports it, its cut) without the portal.
Constraints: page frame pinned by the user's model (Modelo Informe/Imagenes: portada, contraportada, contenido): navy bar #072b58 9 mm, blue band #4078b9 18.3 mm with building line-art, Montserrat. A4 210×297 mm, print through the browser (vector, selectable text). Real data only; an empty value is shown as not reported. The cover and back cover art carry baked text, so live cover text is off by default and switches on when a text-free image is uploaded.

Memorable moment: every indicator reads as a yearbook sheet. A notes column on the left says what is measured, who reports it and how it is calculated; the body carries the anchor figure, its evolution, the campus split and the data table.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A statistical yearbook with a notes column. Each indicator is a documented sheet: explanation in the margin, evidence in the body. It rejects the dashboard pasted onto paper (loose cards, decorative KPIs with no source).

OWN-WORLD: The user's frame: a navy bar with a white logo and the office name, a blue band with the title and the building line-art, white paper. Montserrat. Notes column in pale blue-gray (#eef3f9) with small uppercase labels in navy. Anchor figure in navy Montserrat 800 with tabular numerals. Charts in the band blue, with orange and teal for campuses and a dashed navy line for the total. Tables with thin rules and a navy header row. Page footer with source, cut and number.

STORY: The reader opens the cover, reads the presentation and the contents with page numbers, and enters each report through a chapter opener (two-letter code in its line color, summary, key figures). Page by page it understands the indicator from the margin and verifies it in the chart and the table. The back cover lists the reporting offices.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indicator page. Header from the template (27.5 mm). Below, a 12-column grid inside 16 mm margins: notes column of 3 (WHAT IT MEASURES, REPORTS, HOW IT IS CALCULATED, CUT); body of 9 with the indicator name, the anchor figure at 30 pt with its variation or basis, the evolution chart by year (5 cols) and the campus bars (4 cols), and the full-width campus × years table underneath. Footer at 10 mm.

FORM: Yearbook with a notes column, second on the list of grounded structures (dealt as 4); seed 6ded51eb.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
